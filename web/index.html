OEPL hub installer

Flashes an ESP32-C6 as an OpenEPaperLink hub for the OEPL multihub Home Assistant addon. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, connect the C6 with a USB cable, then click Install. If the board isn't found, hold BOOT while plugging it in.

Wi-Fi hub

The normal choice: place the hub anywhere within Wi-Fi range. After flashing, the installer asks for your Wi-Fi name and password. The hub then shows up as oepl-hub-xxxx.local, TCP port 6639.

Your browser can't do this: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.
